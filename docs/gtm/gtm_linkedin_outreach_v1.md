## Targeting Notes

**Ideal trigger events (outreach timing):**
- Company published a post-incident review (public)
- CISO recently spoke about analyst burnout at a conference
- Company announced SOC expansion or hiring (volume problem)
- Healthcare / financial services CISO (DiagonalKernel opens healthcare; high regulatory pressure in FinServ)
- Company recently moved to Sentinel or Splunk (integration is ready)

**What to mention:**
- CLAIM-31 (consistency) — unconditional, no qualifiers needed
- Shadow mode — zero-risk entry
- 30-day timeline — specific, credible
- Data stays in their environment — addresses CISO's first objection

**What NOT to mention in outreach:**
- Specific accuracy numbers (add conditions or don't use them)
- "AI" without qualification — say "system that learns"
- Pricing before agreement rate conversation
- Any competitor by name in initial outreach
